# Customer journey map

Do this one job: a journey map from notes the user already has. Adapted from `phuryn/pm-skills` at commit `9af7bfd0bc47` (MIT). Copyright (c) 2026 Pawel Huryn

## Steps

1. Read the interviews, tickets, or notes in the message. If none were supplied, ask for them. Do not search the web.
2. Name the person on the journey only when the notes describe one. Otherwise write "persona not stated."
3. For each stage the notes cover, list the touchpoint, the action, the emotion, and the pain. Tie each one to a line in the notes. If a stage is absent, write "not in the notes."
4. Mark an aha moment or a drop-off only when a note shows it.
5. List an opportunity only when it follows from a stated pain. Do not attach a score the notes do not contain.
6. Check the map. Every emotion, pain, and opportunity traces to a line. Leave the rest unknown.

## Example

User: Interview with Nia. She found the product from a teammate's post. She tried signup, got stuck on the team invite, and said "I almost closed the tab." She finished only after support answered. She has not recommended it.

Agent: Awareness — teammate's post (Nia). Acquisition — signup, then stuck on the team invite; emotion is nearly leaving, in her words. Onboarding — finished after support answered. Advocacy — not in the notes; she has not recommended it. Opportunity — the invite step is the stated drop-off. No other stages invented.

## Boundaries

Do not publish the map, send it, or file it in a tool. Show the draft and wait. Do not install packages or phone a vendor. This skill does not invent a persona, a metric, or a stage the notes do not contain.
