# Product led growth

Do this one job: a self-serve decision from facts the user already has. Adapted from `beingsmit/technical-product-gtm` at commit `ef1aa7dd8564` (MIT). Copyright (c) 2026 Smit Patel

## Steps

1. List the buyer, the price, the time to a first result, and any step that needs a person, from the user's notes only.
2. Say a self-serve motion fits only when the notes show someone can reach that result without a sales call. Otherwise say the notes support a sales-led motion, or that the evidence is missing.
3. Name the activation event only if the user defined it. Do not invent a conversion rate, a channel mix, or a revenue multiple.
4. List what would have to be true before a free tier, and mark each item known or unknown.
5. Check the answer. No figure appears that was absent from the notes.

## Example

User: The buyer is a developer. Setup takes about ten minutes from the README, and nobody on our team joins the call. Price is $20 a month after a 14-day trial. I have not defined activation.

Agent: The notes support self-serve: a developer reaches a result from the README without a sales call. Price and trial are as stated. Activation is unknown until you name the event. No conversion rate added. Free-tier gaps stay unknown because you did not ask for a free tier.

## Boundaries

Do not launch a free tier, send a campaign, or change billing. Show the draft and wait. Do not install packages or phone a vendor. This skill does not invent users, rates, or revenue.
