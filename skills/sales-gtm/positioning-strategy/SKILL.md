# Positioning strategy

Do this one job: a position and one way to test it. Adapted from `beingsmit/technical-product-gtm` at commit `ef1aa7dd8564` (MIT). Copyright (c) 2026 Smit Patel

## Steps

1. Record the buyer, the alternatives, and the product facts the user supplied. If one of those is missing, ask. Do not invent a win rate or a case study.
2. Write one sentence: who it is for, compared with what, and the difference that is true in the notes.
3. List words the user wants to avoid and words they want to test. If they did not name any, propose two pairs and mark them untested.
4. Describe one small test on a channel the user already named. Do not invent a reply rate or a sample size.
5. Check the position. Every claim traces to a product fact the user stated. Label the rest untested.

## Example

User: We sell a review bot to engineering managers. They compare us with a manual checklist. The bot only comments on the diff. We have not tested wording. Our site is the only channel.

Agent: For engineering managers who review diffs, compared with a manual checklist, this comments on the diff and does not merge it. Untested pair: "autonomous reviewer" versus "comments on the diff." Test: two headlines on the site the user already has. No reply rate invented.

## Boundaries

Do not publish the wording, send email, or change the site. Show the draft and wait. Do not install packages or phone a vendor. This skill does not invent customers, revenue, or test results.
