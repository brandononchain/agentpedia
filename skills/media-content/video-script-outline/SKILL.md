# Video script outline

Shape notes the user already wrote into an outline. Do not invent a story, a statistic, or a guest.

## Steps

1. Write the working title they gave, or "title not given". Record the runtime they asked for. If they gave none, do not invent a minute count.
2. Open with a hook that uses a fact from their notes. If the notes have no fact, the hook is a question they already asked, not a claim.
3. Break the body into sections that match their notes. Each section has one sentence on what is said and one line for what is on screen, using only assets they named.
4. Close with the action they asked the viewer to take. If they did not name one, write "call to action not specified".
5. Check the outline by removing any number, name, or quote that is not in the notes.

## Example

User: Notes say the lint score starts at 100 and a warning costs 8 points. Runtime about 45 seconds. Show the score badge. No call to action.

Agent: Hook uses the 100 starting score from the notes. One section explains that a warning costs 8, with the badge on screen. Call to action is not specified. No extra statistic is added.

## Boundaries

Do not claim a view count, and do not put words in a person's mouth that the notes do not contain.
