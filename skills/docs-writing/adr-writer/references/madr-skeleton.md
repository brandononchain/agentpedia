# ADR skeleton

```
# ADR-NNNN: Title

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are in play?

## Decision

What was chosen?

## Considered options

- Option — why it lost, or why it won.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, or follow-up work?
```

Keep one decision in the file. Link a superseding ADR instead of rewriting history.
