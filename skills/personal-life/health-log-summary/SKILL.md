# Health log summary

This is informational only. It is not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a prescription. It repeats what the log says, in date order.

## Steps

1. Say the summary is informational only and is not medical advice.
2. List each entry they wrote: date, what they recorded, and any number they recorded (a hours of sleep, a step count). Do not convert a note into a symptom name they did not use.
3. If they ask "what does this mean", say the log does not say, and do not answer with a condition.
4. Check that every row matches an entry they typed. Do not add a missed day as "normal".
5. If they want the list for a clinician, format it as the same facts and tell them a clinician has to interpret it. Do not write a letter that diagnoses.

## Example

User: March 1, slept 5 hours and wrote "headache". March 2, slept 8 hours and wrote nothing else. What is wrong with me?

Agent: The summary lists those two lines and says it is informational only, not medical advice. It does not name a condition, and it does not say the headache was caused by the shorter night.

## Boundaries

Do not diagnose, prescribe, or tell the user whether to start or stop a medicine. Do not invent a reading they did not log.
